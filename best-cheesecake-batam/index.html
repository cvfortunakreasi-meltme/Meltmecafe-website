Frequently asked questions

What is the Dubai Chewy Cheezmelt?

A combination of choco cheezmelt, pistachio kunafa, mochi, and chocolate sauce. It's a limited-edition item, so availability is limited — check with us on WhatsApp before you visit if you want to be sure.

What is the OG Cheezmelt like?

A soft-baked basque cheesecake square with a caramelized top and a melt-in-the-mouth centre. Available by the slice (40k) or whole (70k).

How far is it from Harbour Bay Ferry Terminal?

About a 2-minute walk. Exit through the terminal's main entrance, walk straight toward Citywalk Harbour Bay, and Melt Me is at unit 8-10.

Is Melt Me halal?

Yes, the entire menu is made in a halal-friendly kitchen.

What are Melt Me's opening hours?

Daily, 10:00 AM – 11:00 PM.
